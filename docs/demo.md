# Product demo

The 70-second film shows ThreadLume through real browser recordings: expanding a summary, opening a mind-map branch and copying an outline, seeking a video chapter, and explicitly stopping and retrying an AI request. The 10-second README preview uses the same captures. Full video: [watch or download the MP4](https://github.com/devon-kong/ThreadLume/releases/download/v0.12.0/ThreadLume-demo.mp4).

## What the footage establishes

Recorded on 22 October 2026 in the existing installed follow-up environment. Repository baseline at capture: `11addb53087c06d44d353829426ae58ec09307ea`; the local build manifest was 0.12.0. This baseline does not prove the installed bundle's exact Git SHA. The published v0.12.0 ZIP remains its tagged build; this recording includes the later UI follow-up documented in [showcase validation](showcase-validation.md).

Article summary and map interactions use existing completed results. The first video chapter click moved the original playing video from approximately 26.29 to 54.05 seconds, with playback continuing. The controls segment explicitly selects Chinese output, stops the request and retries once. Its later completed result is a real screenshot; **“Later · waiting time omitted”** marks the omitted wait. Product controls are English; native X chrome remains in the browser's Chinese locale.

Camera zooms, labels and click rings are editorial annotations. Brief final-frame holds support reading; no model output or product control was rewritten. This is a selected workflow demo, not a benchmark, first-install validation, or claim of universal compatibility. Video chapters depend on available native captions. See the [source-quality and caption-coverage caveats](showcase-validation.md#output-quality-samples).
